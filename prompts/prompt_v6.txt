Separate two kinds of termination or notice right that are easy to conflate, regardless of whether both state a fixed number of days:
(a) ROLLING — the right can be exercised at any time after a trigger event (e.g. "at any time after the Initial Term," "at any time, for any reason," "at any time, without cause," "cancellable at any time... upon N days' notice"). There is no single calendar cutoff even when the notice period itself is a precise number of days, because the window to act never closes — it just keeps recurring. Language such as "at any time" or "at any time... before the expiry date" is a ROLLING signal and controls the classification even when the same sentence also references an expiry or renewal date — a clause almost always has to say notice comes "before" the contract ends, since a contract cannot be cancelled after it is already over; that phrase alone does not create a bounded pre-expiry window. Label this notice_type "rolling termination" and deadline_calculable "No."
(b) FIXED — notice must fall within a bounded window that closes on or before a specific, recurring expiry or renewal date, and the clause does NOT also say the right may be exercised at any earlier time (e.g. "at least 30 days before the expiration of the then-current term," with no "at any time" language). This has exactly one discrete last-possible day each cycle. Label this notice_type "non-renewal" (if it stops a renewal) or "renewal election" (if it is an affirmative opt-in to renew), and deadline_calculable "Yes" whenever the relevant expiry or election date is established by the contract — this applies equally to a deadline to elect renewal and to a deadline to prevent it; do not restrict "calculable" to non-renewal notices only.
The test is never "is a day-count stated," and it is never "does the clause mention an expiry date" — both rolling and fixed clauses usually do both. The test is whether the clause grants an "at any time" right (rolling) or bounds the notice to a single closing window per cycle with no "at any time" language (fixed). When genuinely unsure which applies, say so in deadline_reason rather than defaulting to fixed.
