AMENDED AND RESTATED ADMINISTRATION AGREEMENT

 

This Administration Agreement (“Agreement”) dated as of September 24, 2026, and effective as of September 25, 2026, is by and between State Street Bank and Trust Company, a Massachusetts trust company (the “Administrator”), and The Arbitrage Funds, a Delaware statutory trust (the “Trust”).

 

WHEREAS, the Trust is an open-end management investment company currently comprised of multiple series (each, a “Fund” and collectively, the “Funds”), and is registered with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”) by means of a registration statement (“Registration Statement”) under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (“1933 Act”), and the Investment Company Act of 1940, as amended (the “1940 Act”);

 

WHEREAS, Trust and the Administrator had entered into an Administration Agreement, dated as of April 17, 2013 (as amended and in effect, the “Arbitrage Administration Agreement”);

 

WHEREAS, AltShares Trust, an open-end management company comprised of two series, AltShares Merger Arbitrage ETF and AltShares Event-Driven ETF, and the Administrator had previously entered into a separate Administration Agreement, dated as of February 7, 2020 (as amended and in effect, the “AltShares Administration Agreement”);

 

WHEREAS, pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Reorganization intended to qualify as a tax-free reorganization under Section 368(a)(1)(F) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, effective as of September 25, 2026, AltShares Merger Arbitrage ETF and AltShares Event-Driven ETF, each then a series of AltShares Trust, transferred all of their respective assets and liabilities to, and were reorganized into, newly established series of the Trust bearing the same respective names, and as a result of such reorganization, AltShares Merger Arbitrage ETF and AltShares Event-Driven ETF each became a series of the Trust;

 

WHEREAS, The Arbitrage Funds and the Administrator desire to replace the Arbitrage Administration Agreement with this Amended and Restated Administration Agreement;

 

WHEREAS, the Trust desires to retain the Administrator to furnish certain administrative services to the Trust, and the Administrator is willing to furnish such services, on the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.

 

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the premises and mutual covenants herein contained, the parties hereto agree as follows:
